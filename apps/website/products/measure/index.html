Technical information

Reference the component

Start with the drawing datum and clamping surfaces. The fixture should locate the part without obstructing the measurement path.

Match the instrument

Instrument range, resolution and calibration are selected for the application. The bench dimensions do not imply a measurement accuracy.

Keep the sequence clear

Review loading, clamping, measurement and unloading as a sequence. Record the result format and any traceability requirements.
