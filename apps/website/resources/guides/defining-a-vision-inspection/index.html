Before choosing a vision inspection system

Define the inspection decision before comparing cameras.

Collect meaningful samples

Include accepted parts, known defects and variations in material, colour and presentation. Record which differences should change the result.

State the acceptance boundary

Specify the inspected features, allowable variation and how a disputed result will be reviewed. Align the inspection method with the drawing requirement.

Include the handling sequence

Review loading, settling, triggering, inspection and unloading. A camera exposure is only one part of the station cycle.

Inspect vision station

A guarded benchtop cell for camera-based inspection of individual components. A defined fixture, adjustable camera bridge and controlled lighting provide a starting point for a repeatable inspection task.
