Engineering the connections that matter.

Vorntek develops inspection equipment and modular production systems. We bring mechanical engineering, workholding and controls together around the component and the operation.

One process, several disciplines.

A camera, a fixture and a conveyor each solve part of a problem. Their timing, interfaces and operating conditions determine how the complete cell works. We consider those connections from the beginning.

Build from representative parts.

Drawings and representative samples help define the task. Surface variation, dimensional limits and the handling sequence are reviewed before equipment configuration and acceptance criteria are agreed.

A clear boundary for integration.

Mechanical transfer, field wiring, network access and result exchange need named responsibilities. We make those interfaces part of the engineering discussion, along with installation, commissioning and support.
