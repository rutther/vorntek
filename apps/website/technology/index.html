A working system starts at the interfaces.

Mechanical stability, controlled measurement conditions and well-defined signals turn individual devices into a useful production cell.

Workholding and repeatability

Define the component datum, contact surfaces and loading sequence. Fixtures must position the part consistently while leaving access for the tool, sensor and operator.

Optics and measurement conditions

Part finish, ambient light, working distance and the tolerance all influence instrument selection. Evaluate representative variations rather than relying on a catalogue figure alone.

Signals and information exchange

Agree trigger, busy, ready and result meanings at the interface. Define how timestamps, product identifiers, interrupted connections and retries are handled by both sides.

Access, guarding and maintenance

Consider normal operation, clearing a fault, tool changes and service access separately. The final equipment configuration and protective measures require a project-specific assessment.
